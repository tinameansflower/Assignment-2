  <summary> <h6> <strong> My Contributions </strong> </h6> </summary>
  <p> The yearbook committee was a senior course and one of the best decisions I made. I hosted a legacy project called candy grams; it was a fundraiser. I got to design pages, be the editor-in-chief of the overall yearbook, got to talk to teachers like they were an actual client, practiced my photography skills, and really be involved in school spirit. Additionally, the yearbook committee didn’t really feel like a class; if anything, it felt like a hobby, something I enjoy, which is exactly the reason it had such an impact on me. I met so many incredible people, and this environment is what ultimately led me to pursue a UX design degree.  
    </p>

      <summary> <h6> <strong> What I enjoyed </strong> </h6> </summary>
 
  <p>I met incredible people, teachers I still talk to today, and I found a teacher I can really open up to. My experience was nothing but good. I loved the yearbook, designing pages, doing what I can to help others, being part of communities and clubs; it was an absolute blast. This product was sold to all students, selling to hundreds; though the sales were kept to teachers only, it was an accomplishment and my first-ever client-based course. <details> 
      <summary> <h6> <strong> Favourite Memory </strong> </h6> </summary>

 <p> My favourite memory was when I co-hosted a Candy Gram event at school with four people for charity. The earnings went to victim services, with around $170 raised and 260 candy grams purchased. This was the first time I planned an event for the community. 

Throughout the project, my tasks included scheduling meetings, ensuring that we had all of the necessary materials, and planning the work schedule ahead of time, as this was one of the most successful events past students had conducted. Not only that, but I got to design the cards; it was incredible seeing all the people buying candy grams for their loved ones. We also hand-assembled them, and it was an amazing festive experience because we sang karaoke to promote it too.  </p>
  </details>
