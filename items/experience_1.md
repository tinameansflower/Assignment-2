  <summary> <h6> <strong> My Contributions </strong> </h6> </summary>
  <p>I was a UX researcher collaborating with the Student Union to locate why students lacked access to information and to produce solutions on how that could be remedied. I made sure we were on the correct track, looking for what we needed to be looking for, and interviewed students along with the client themselves to figure out what they think is going on and how they feel the system is, along with possible issues that they encountered or frequent questions they get asked. I analyzed the data collected from interviews to narrow down the issue before presenting it with my group members and brainstorming together on possible solutions based on external research (looking at other SU university webs and Google searches) and internal research (student voices and interviews, knowing if they know student union had a website or used it at all).
